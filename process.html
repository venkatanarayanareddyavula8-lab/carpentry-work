OUR PROCESS

FROM IDEA TO INSTALLATION

Every custom furniture project follows a thoughtful journey. From the first conversation to the final installation, each stage is carefully planned, crafted, and refined to create furniture that is made specifically for you.

01
CONSULTATION

Understanding Your Vision

We begin with a detailed conversation about your ideas, lifestyle, preferences, functional needs, and the furniture you want to create.

Your vision begins with a conversation.
02
UNDERSTANDING YOUR SPACE

Designed Around Your Space

We study your available space, layout, existing interiors, movement, and everyday requirements to understand how your custom furniture should fit and function.

Designed around the way you live.
03
DESIGN CONCEPT

Turning Ideas Into Design

Your vision is transformed into a thoughtful design concept. We consider proportions, functionality, style, comfort, and details that make the piece truly yours.

Your ideas take shape through thoughtful design.
04
MEASUREMENTS

Precision & Planning

Accurate measurements create the foundation for a perfect fit. Every width, height, depth, clearance, and placement detail is carefully considered before production begins.

Precision creates the perfect fit.
05
MATERIAL SELECTION

Choosing The Right Materials

We help you select suitable wood, materials, finishes, colours, textures, and hardware based on your preferred look, durability, maintenance, and project requirements.

Materials selected with purpose.
06
FINAL QUOTE

Clear Project Pricing

Once the design and materials are confirmed, we prepare a clear and transparent quotation covering the project scope, materials, craftsmanship, finishing, and related work.

Clear planning. Transparent pricing.
07
DESIGN APPROVAL

Final Design Confirmation

Before craftsmanship begins, we review the final design and important project details with you. Dimensions, materials, finishes, functionality, and specifications are carefully confirmed.

Your vision, confirmed before production.
08
WOODWORKING

Where Craftsmanship Begins

Our craftsmen carefully select, cut, shape, join, and prepare each component. Every stage is handled with precision and attention to the character and quality of the material.

Skilled hands bring the design to life.
09
ASSEMBLY

Built With Precision

Individual components are brought together to create the finished structure. Joints, fittings, alignment, strength, and details are carefully checked during assembly.

Every component comes together with purpose.
10
FINISHING

Refined Surface Details

The furniture is carefully sanded, prepared, and finished to enhance its natural character. The final treatment creates a refined surface while protecting the piece for everyday use.

The final character begins to emerge.
11
QUALITY INSPECTION

Every Detail Checked

Before leaving our workshop, every piece receives a detailed quality inspection. We check construction, alignment, fittings, finishing, proportions, and overall appearance.

Every detail deserves attention.
12
DELIVERY & INSTALLATION

From Our Workshop To Your Space

Your completed furniture is carefully prepared for delivery and transported to your location. Our team positions and installs the piece carefully so everything is ready to enjoy.

Carefully delivered. Beautifully installed.
