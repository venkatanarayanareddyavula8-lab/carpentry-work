Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026
01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our Custom Furniture Maker & Carpentry Workshop collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by visitors and customers when using our website and services.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us, requesting a custom furniture quote, or submitting an enquiry.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address or project location
  • Furniture requirements
  • Dimensions and measurements
  • Budget preferences
  • Material preferences
  • Project descriptions
  • Reference images uploaded through the enquiry form
03

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our website may be used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to furniture enquiries
  • Preparing custom furniture quotations
  • Understanding your design requirements
  • Communicating about your project
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving our website and services
  • Managing custom orders and workshop services
04

Custom Project Information

When you submit a custom furniture enquiry, you may provide project-specific information such as dimensions, sketches, design ideas, material preferences, room requirements, and reference photographs.

This information is used to understand your project and provide appropriate design, quotation, manufacturing, delivery, and installation services.

05

Reference Images

If you upload reference images through our enquiry form, those images may be reviewed by our team to understand your preferred furniture style, design, materials, or finishing requirements.

Please avoid uploading photographs containing sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your furniture project.

06

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to operate our services, process your enquiry, complete an order, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Any service providers involved in supporting our website or business operations should only receive information necessary for their specific service.

07

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

10

We retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to provide requested services, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

11

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of certain information.
